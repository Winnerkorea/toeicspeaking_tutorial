## Part 1 - Quick Answers

### Q. How long do you use the internet every day?
**A.** I usually use the internet for about 1 hour every day.

### Q. What kind of books do you enjoy reading?
**A.** I enjoy reading essays because I can get knowledge from them.

### Q. Do you enjoy online shopping?
**A.** I enjoy online shopping. It is usually cheaper than offline shopping.

### Q. Would you consider spending your leisure time at the park?
**A.** I would consider spending my leisure time at the park. I like running with my friends.

### Q. Do you prefer to buy formal clothes or casual clothes?
**A.** I prefer to buy casual clothes because they are comfortable and convenient.

### Q. How long have you had your bed?
**A.** I have had my bed for 6 years.

### Q. When was the last time you visited a museum?
**A.** The last time I visited a museum was three years ago.

### Q. Which of the following do you think is most important when purchasing a bag?
**A.** I think price is the most important because I can save money.

### Q. How many people do you call on a daily basis?
**A.** I call about 2 people on a daily basis.

### Q. How many times have you been to a movie theater in the last six months?
**A.** I have been to a movie theater about 10 times in the last six months.

## Part 2 - Subway vs Bus / Books

### Q. Do you prefer to take the subway or a bus? Why?
**A.** I prefer to take the subway because I don't have to worry about traffic. Actually, I took a bus to school yesterday, and I was late for class because it was rush hour.

### Q. What kind of books do you usually read?
**A.** I usually read comic books. That's because they make me feel very excited. Especially, I like the series "Spider-Man".

## Part 3 - E-books

### Q. What are the advantages of e-books?
**A.** E-books are very convenient and comfortable. It is easy to carry them anywhere. When I travel, I can read books on my phone.

### Q. What are the advantages of e-books? (Another answer)
**A.** The biggest advantage of e-books is that they are cheaper than printed books.

## Part 4 - Smartphone

### Q4. Have you ever bought a product using your smartphone? If so, what did you buy?

**Korean:**
> 저는 스마트폰으로 물건을 사본 적이 있습니다. 그것은 책이었습니다.
> 서점에서 세일 중이라 저는 책을 몇 권 구입했습니다.
> 저의 스마트폰으로 쉽게 온라인에 접속해서 물건을 구입했습니다.

**English:**
> Yes, I have bought a product using my smartphone. I bought some books about 3 days ago. There was a bookstore having a sale, and its books were cheaper than other online stores, so I bought some books on my phone.

### Q5. How often do you use your smartphone? And when do you usually use it?

**Korean:**
> 저는 매일 스마트폰을 사용합니다. 저는 그것을 매일 밤낮으로 이용합니다. 저는 스마트폰을 이용해서 개발을 하고 업무를 수행합니다.

**English:**
> I use my smartphone every day, day and night. In my office, I use it to take pictures of products and test results, and I use development apps for work.

### Q6. What are some disadvantages of using smartphones?

**Korean:**
> 스마트폰의 단점이 몇 가지 있습니다.
> 먼저, 스마트폰을 사용하면 쉽게 인터넷 쇼핑을 하면서 돈을 절약할 수 없습니다.
> 그리고 스마트폰을 사용하면 친구들과 어울리는 대신 많은 시간을 스마트폰에 사용하게 됩니다.
> 더 나아가 스마트폰 사용은 당신의 여가시간을 쉽게 써버리게 하고 결국 시간을 낭비하게 할 수 있습니다.

**English:**
> There are some disadvantages of using smartphones. First, I can't save money because online shopping is too easy. Second, instead of spending time with friends, I spend a lot of time on my smartphone. Furthermore, using a smartphone can waste my free time.
