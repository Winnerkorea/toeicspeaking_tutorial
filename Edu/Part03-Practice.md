# 토익스피킹 파트 3 연습문제 30개

아래 10개 세트는 파트 3 연습용으로 만든 문제입니다. 한 세트에서 같은 주제에 관한 5·6·7번을 차례로 답하세요.

1. 영어 질문을 하나씩 듣거나 읽으세요. 질문이 끝나면 3초 동안 준비합니다.
2. 5·6번은 15초, 7번은 30초 안에 답하며 녹음하세요.
3. 답한 뒤 예시 답변을 보고 질문이 요구한 정보를 모두 담았는지 확인하세요. 같은 질문에 자신의 경험을 넣어 다시 말해 보세요.

## 문제

### 세트 01 동네 카페

Imagine you are taking part in a telephone interview about neighborhood cafés.

- **5번 · 15초** How often do you go to a café near your home?
- **6번 · 15초** What do you usually order at a café, and why?
- **7번 · 30초** Would you recommend your favorite café to someone who wants to study? Why or why not?

### 세트 02 출퇴근

Imagine you are taking part in a telephone interview about commuting.

- **5번 · 15초** When do you usually leave home for work or school?
- **6번 · 15초** How do you usually get to work or school, and how long does it take?
- **7번 · 30초** Which would you prefer for your commute, driving or taking public transportation? Why?

### 세트 03 도서관

Imagine you are taking part in a telephone interview about libraries.

- **5번 · 15초** Can you walk to a library from your home?
- **6번 · 15초** When did you last visit a library, and what did you do there?
- **7번 · 30초** How would you encourage more people to visit your local library?

### 세트 04 선물

Imagine you are taking part in a telephone interview about giving gifts.

- **5번 · 15초** Who did you last buy a gift for, and what did you buy?
- **6번 · 15초** Have you ever made a gift for someone? If so, what did you make?
- **7번 · 30초** Would you rather give someone a practical gift or a gift they can enjoy for a day? Why?

### 세트 05 운동

Imagine you are taking part in a telephone interview about exercise.

- **5번 · 15초** How many times a week do you exercise?
- **6번 · 15초** Do you usually exercise alone? Why or why not?
- **7번 · 30초** What would make it easier for you to exercise regularly? Explain your answer.

### 세트 06 온라인 쇼핑

Imagine you are taking part in a telephone interview about online shopping.

- **5번 · 15초** Do you usually compare prices before buying something online?
- **6번 · 15초** What kind of products do you usually buy online, and why?
- **7번 · 30초** How do you decide whether to trust an online store you've never used before?

### 세트 07 주말 여행

Imagine you are taking part in a telephone interview about weekend trips.

- **5번 · 15초** How long do your weekend trips usually last?
- **6번 · 15초** Where did you go on your most recent weekend trip, and who went with you?
- **7번 · 30초** Would you take a weekend trip without booking a place to stay in advance? Why or why not?

### 세트 08 요리

Imagine you are taking part in a telephone interview about cooking at home.

- **5번 · 15초** What do you usually cook when you don't have much time?
- **6번 · 15초** How much time do you usually spend making dinner, and when do you start?
- **7번 · 30초** Describe a dish you enjoy cooking at home. Explain what you like about making it.

### 세트 09 영화

Imagine you are taking part in a telephone interview about movies.

- **5번 · 15초** Have you watched a movie at a theater in the past month?
- **6번 · 15초** How often do you watch movies at home, and who do you usually watch them with?
- **7번 · 30초** Would you rather watch a comedy or a documentary with friends? Why?

### 세트 10 새로운 기술 배우기

Imagine you are taking part in a telephone interview about learning a new skill.

- **5번 · 15초** What skill would you like to learn this year?
- **6번 · 15초** Have you ever taken a class online? If so, what did you study?
- **7번 · 30초** How would you help a friend who wants to practice a new skill but has very little free time?

## 예시 답변과 확인할 내용

### 세트 01 동네 카페 답변

**5번 · How** · 빈도와 단위

> I go to a café near my home about twice a week. I usually stop by on my way home from work.

**6번 · WH** · 메뉴와 이유

> I usually order an iced Americano because I like its strong flavor. I often have it with a small cookie.

**7번 · Yes/No** · 추천 여부와 공부하기 좋은 이유 및 경험

> Yes, I would recommend it. It has large tables and plenty of power outlets, so it's easy to work on a laptop. I studied there for two hours last Sunday, and the café stayed quiet the whole time.

### 세트 02 출퇴근 답변

**5번 · WH** · 출발 시각

> I usually leave home at around seven thirty. That gives me enough time to catch the bus before it gets crowded.

**6번 · How** · 이동 수단과 소요 시간

> I usually take the subway. It takes about thirty minutes, including a short walk from the station.

**7번 · WH** · 선택과 이유

> I'd prefer taking public transportation. I can relax on the train instead of concentrating on traffic, and I don't have to find a parking space. For example, I usually read a few pages of a book on the way to work.

### 세트 03 도서관 답변

**5번 · Yes/No** · 도보 가능 여부

> Yes, I can. There's a public library about ten minutes away on foot, right next to the park.

**6번 · WH** · 방문 시점과 한 일

> I went to the library last Saturday. I returned a novel and borrowed a travel guide for my upcoming trip.

**7번 · How** · 방법과 실행 예시

> I would organize free weekend workshops. For example, the library could offer a short class on how to use a smartphone, then invite participants to explore its other services. People who enjoy the class might come back for another event.

### 세트 04 선물 답변

**5번 · WH** · 받는 사람과 선물

> I last bought a gift for my sister. I got her a reusable water bottle because she often goes hiking.

**6번 · Yes/No** · 경험 여부와 만든 선물

> Yes, I have. I made a photo album for my parents and filled it with pictures from our family trips.

**7번 · A/B 선택** · 두 선택지 중 하나와 이유

> I'd rather give a practical gift because the person can use it again and again. Last year, I gave a friend a lunch box with separate compartments. She brings it to work almost every day, so I know it was a good choice.

### 세트 05 운동 답변

**5번 · How** · 주당 횟수

> I exercise three times a week. I usually go for a run on two weekdays and take a long walk on Sunday.

**6번 · Yes/No** · 평소 행동과 이유

> No, I usually exercise with a friend. Having someone to meet makes it easier for me to stick to my schedule.

**7번 · WH** · 도움이 될 변화와 구체적인 설명

> A gym closer to home would make it easier for me to exercise regularly. My current gym is a thirty-minute bus ride away, so I often skip it when I get home late. If there were one nearby, I could fit in a short workout after dinner.

### 세트 06 온라인 쇼핑 답변

**5번 · Yes/No** · 비교 여부

> Yes, I do. I usually check two or three websites and compare the total cost, including shipping.

**6번 · WH** · 제품 종류와 이유

> I usually buy books online because I can find titles that aren't available at my local bookstore.

**7번 · How** · 판단 방법과 기준

> I check recent reviews and read the return policy. Reviews can show whether the store delivers on time, and the return policy tells me what to do if something arrives damaged. If I can't find either one, I usually shop somewhere else.

### 세트 07 주말 여행 답변

**5번 · How** · 여행 기간

> They usually last two days. I leave on Saturday morning and get home on Sunday evening.

**6번 · WH** · 장소와 동행인

> I went to a small town on the coast last month. My cousin came with me, and we spent most of the trip by the water.

**7번 · Yes/No** · 여부와 이유 및 경험

> No, I wouldn't. I like to know where I'll stay before I leave, especially if I'm arriving late. On a recent trip, several hotels near the station were full, so I was glad I'd booked a room ahead of time.

### 세트 08 요리 답변

**5번 · WH** · 메뉴

> I usually make fried rice. I can use leftover rice and whatever vegetables I have in the fridge.

**6번 · How** · 소요 시간과 시작 시각

> I usually spend about half an hour making dinner. I start around six thirty, right after I get home.

**7번 · 묘사** · 요리와 특징 및 만드는 즐거움

> I enjoy making vegetable soup. I usually use onions, carrots, and potatoes, but I can add any vegetables I have on hand. I like making it because one pot gives me enough soup for several meals, and it tastes even better the next day.

### 세트 09 영화 답변

**5번 · Yes/No** · 경험 여부와 관련 정보

> Yes, I have. I saw an animated movie with my brother two weeks ago.

**6번 · How** · 빈도와 함께 보는 사람

> I watch movies at home about once a week. I usually watch them with my roommate on Friday nights.

**7번 · A/B 선택** · 선택과 이유 및 예시

> I'd choose a comedy. When I watch something with friends, I enjoy laughing together and talking about the funny scenes afterward. Last Friday, we watched a comedy at my apartment and kept quoting our favorite lines for the rest of the evening.

### 세트 10 새로운 기술 배우기 답변

**5번 · WH** · 배우고 싶은 기술

> I'd like to learn how to edit videos. I want to make short clips of my weekend trips.

**6번 · Yes/No** · 수강 경험 여부

> No, I haven't. The last class I took was a photography class at my local community center.

**7번 · How** · 지원 방법과 구체적인 실행 계획

> I'd help my friend make a short practice schedule. For example, if they wanted to learn guitar, I'd suggest practicing one chord for ten minutes after dinner three times a week. I'd also check in on Sundays to see what they'd learned.
